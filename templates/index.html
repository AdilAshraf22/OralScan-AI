01

How to photograph your mouth

01

Enable the torch

Turn on your phone flashlight before opening the camera. The oral cavity is dark and requires additional lighting for a clear image.

02

Use a mirror

Stand in front of a bathroom mirror and use the reflection to aim your phone camera accurately at the inner cheek or tongue.

03

Open wide

Open your mouth as wide as comfortable. Focus on any abnormal area — white patches, red spots, or persistent ulcers.

04

Capture clearly

Hold your phone steady and photograph the area. Ensure the region of concern is in focus and well-illuminated.
